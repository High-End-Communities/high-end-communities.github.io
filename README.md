# High End Communities

**Buy and sell high-end items within the community — Estd 2024**

High End Communities is a members' marketplace where people list items for sale and share them in the community WhatsApp group.

🌐 **Catalogue (everyone, read-only):** https://high-end-communities.github.io
➕ **Post a listing (sellers):** https://high-end-communities.github.io/post.html
🔐 **Admin portal (login):** https://high-end-communities.github.io/admin.html

## Categories

Every listing is marked **New** or **Used**. Prices can be entered in **EGP or US dollars**; motorcycle engines in **cc or ci** (cubic inches).

| | Category | What you can list |
|---|---|---|
| 🏍️ | **Motorcycles** | Motorcycles, safety gear (helmets, jackets, gloves, boots, intercoms) and accessories (exhausts, LED lights, luggage, crash bars…) |
| ⌚ | **Watches** | Luxury and collectible watches (4,000 – 2,000,000 EGP) |
| 🚗 | **Cars** | Cars of every type, plus car parts & accessories (wheels, body, engine, lights, interior, electronics, performance) |
| 🐎 | **Equine** | Horses, plus saddles, tack, rugs, riding wear, stable equipment and horse trailers |
| 🛥️ | **Yachts & boats** | Yachts, speedboats, sailboats, jet skis, fishing boats, plus marine parts & accessories (engines, electronics, safety, trailers) |
| 🎨 | **Modern & art** | Paintings, sculpture, photography, prints, furniture & design, collectibles |
| 🏠 | **Real estate** | Apartments, villas, townhouses, chalets, penthouses, land, commercial |

## How to list an item

1. Open the **Post a listing** link. Tip: paste your WhatsApp message in **Quick fill** and the form fills itself.
2. Choose the category and take or choose 1–4 photos. Photos are reduced to a small size automatically.
3. Fill in the required details: name, brand or type, condition (New / Used), price and phone number. Prices must be within the range set for that category.
4. Tick **I accept the terms** (below).
5. Send it for review. You get a **6-digit seller code**: keep it, you need it to mark your item as sold.
6. Once the admin approves it, it appears in the catalogue and can be shared to WhatsApp as a message, flyer image or PDF.
